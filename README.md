## How I work

- **Start with a mechanism:** use economic reasoning to define what should be
  tested before optimizing a metric.
- **Separate exploration from validation:** human judgment and LLM assistance
  expand the search space; deterministic code owns measurement and evidence.
- **Model implementation early:** timing, exposure, turnover, financing,
  liquidity, and capacity are part of the research question.
- **Keep disconfirming evidence:** weak sleeves, failed stress cases, and
  limitations remain visible because they improve decisions.

releases. They provide enough research design, aggregate evidence, failure
analysis, and implementation detail for serious review while keeping reusable
discovery instructions, exact signals, and trading paths private.

investment advice, or a guarantee of future returns.
