### LLM-Driven Alpha Discovery

Built a quantitative R&D system that transforms broad market questions into
structured factor candidates, evaluates them through a consistent research
pipeline, and compounds successful experiments into a reusable alpha-discovery
capability.

The system combines LLM-powered hypothesis expansion with chronological
testing, portfolio translation, cost analysis, capacity diagnostics, and
versioned evidence—connecting generative intelligence to institutional
quantitative research.

**Selected result:** deployed across 257 exchange-traded instruments, one
discovered factor produced **13.83% net CAGR**, **1.55 Sharpe**, and **−4.25%
maximum drawdown** over 1,123 walk-forward out-of-sample observations at 5 bp
one-way cost. The same factor's higher-risk expression produced **27.80% net
CAGR** with a **1.52 Sharpe**.

### Systematic Equity Alpha

Designed an end-to-end U.S. large-cap absolute-return system combining
dual-tail cross-sectional ML, market-neutral portfolio construction, a
separately controlled directional risk overlay, next-open execution, and

**Selected result:** **23.49% CAGR** and **1.24 Sharpe** after 5 bp one-way

### Crypto Relative Value

Designed a point-in-time perpetual-futures research system combining sparse
mean reversion and cross-sectional funding carry within a shared portfolio,
risk, execution, and accounting engine.

lifecycle-aware research universe, with whole-pair execution, realized funding,

**Selected result:** **22.08% CAGR** and **1.62 Sharpe** in development at

returned **+4.12%**.
